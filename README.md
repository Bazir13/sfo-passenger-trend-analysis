# SFO Passenger Traffic & Airline Performance Analysis (2018–2026)

An exploratory data analysis examining passenger volume trends, airline market share distribution, and post-pandemic recovery dynamics at San Francisco International Airport (SFO) from January 2018 through August 2026.

## Project Overview
* **Timeframe Analyzed:** January 2018 – August 2026
* **Primary Data Source:** Public aviation datasets (SFO Airport / data.gov)
* **Core Focus Areas:**
  * Carrier market share and concentration (highlighting United Airlines' operational footprint).
  * COVID-19 shock analysis and multi-year recovery modeling.
  * Structural divergence in domestic vs. international travel demand.

---

## Executive Summary & Key Insights

* **Carrier Dominance:** United Airlines dominates SFO traffic, capturing **41%** of all passengers across the analyzed timeline, surpassing all other carriers combined.
* **Pandemic Shock:** Monthly traffic collapsed by over **95%** between March and April 2020, dropping sharply from roughly 4 million to under 140,000 passengers in a single month.
* **Recovery Timeline:** Total monthly passenger volume took nearly 3 years to consistently return to pre-pandemic baselines (4M+ monthly), stabilizing around 2022–2023.
* **Domestic vs. International Divergence:** Domestic travel rebounded rapidly, nearing 2019 volumes by 2022. Conversely, international traffic experienced a permanent structural shift, remaining below its pre-pandemic peak through 2026.

## Strategic Recommendations & Business Implications

* **Strategic Diversification for Dominant Carriers:** United Airlines captures 41% of SFO's total passenger volume. Secondary carriers and airport planners should target underserved regional or international routes to capture spilled demand and improve competitive pricing.
* **Targeted International Route Incentivization:** Domestic travel fully rebounded by 2022, but international passenger volume experienced a structural shift. Airport authorities and partner airlines should collaborate on targeted marketing campaigns and landing fee incentives to accelerate international recovery.
* **Resilient Operational Planning for Macro Shocks:** The 95% traffic collapse in early 2020 highlights the need for dynamic capacity scaling and flexible cost structures to protect margins during severe demand downturns.
